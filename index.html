About

I am a hospitality educator and researcher completing my PhD in Hospitality Management at Iowa State University, where I also teach. My work sits at the intersection of food safety compliance and hospitality workforce development, grounded in more than a decade of hotel front office management and nine years of teaching hospitality operations across lodging, foodservice, and marketing.

I am a lecturer at Takoradi Technical University in Ghana and I hold the Certified Hospitality Educator credential along with ServSafe Manager and Instructor certifications.

Research

My research develops evidence based frameworks that connect food safety policy to practical workforce training, so that hospitality operators have inclusive strategies that improve compliance and expand who can participate in the industry. It runs along four connected lines of inquiry.

Inclusive training for employees with disabilities

How foodservice managers train employees with disabilities on food safety, and what equitable, accessible training looks like in practice.

Food safety compliance in commercial foodservice

How training materials and food safety guidance can be made clearer and more usable so they actually change behavior on the floor.

Careers and professional identity in hospitality education

Entrepreneurship, career development, and how students form professional identities as they enter the industry.

Food culture and sustainability in West Africa

Culinary heritage, food practices, and sustainable foodservice in West African contexts.

Doctoral dissertation

Assessing food safety training for individuals with disabilities in the US foodservice industry

A national mixed methods study of 315 foodservice managers, grounded in the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices framework. Advised by Dr. Anirudh R. Naig.

Insights

Short writing on food safety, disability inclusion, and workforce practice in foodservice.
