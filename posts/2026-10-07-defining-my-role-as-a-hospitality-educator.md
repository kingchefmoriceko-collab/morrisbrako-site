title: "Defining My Role as a Hospitality Educator"
date: 2026-10-07
summary: "I argue that a clear professional identity strengthens teaching, research, and industry engagement in hospitality education."
---

![](images/headers/workforce.svg)

The landscape of hospitality education is shifting rapidly, and I find that a well-articulated professional identity is essential for navigating these changes. When I reflect on my responsibilities, I see three intersecting pillars: teaching, research, and industry collaboration. Each pillar demands attention, yet they often compete for time and resources.

I believe that clarity about my role helps me allocate effort more effectively and models the same clarity for my students. When I prioritize continuous learning, I stay current with food safety standards and emerging inclusion practices, which in turn enriches classroom discussions. Likewise, maintaining active ties with industry partners ensures that my research remains grounded in real-world challenges and that my students receive relevant, practice-oriented instruction.

To sustain a meaningful professional identity, I propose three guiding principles. First, commit to lifelong learning that bridges theory and practice. Second, engage regularly with professional communities, both academic and industry, to exchange ideas and resources. Third, align personal values with institutional missions, especially around inclusion and workforce development. By adhering to these principles, I aim to contribute more effectively to the next generation of hospitality professionals.
