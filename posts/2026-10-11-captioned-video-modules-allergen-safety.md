---
title: "Captioned Video Modules for Allergen Safety Training"
date: 2026-10-11
summary: "Captioned video instruction can make allergen safety training more accessible for hearing-impaired foodservice employees."
---

![](images/headers/inclusion.svg)

Allergen safety is a critical component of foodservice operations, yet standard training often relies on spoken explanations that exclude hearing-impaired staff. I believe that an inclusive approach must provide equivalent learning pathways for every employee, regardless of ability.

Captioned video modules offer a practical solution. By presenting the same information in both audio and synchronized text, these modules allow hearing-impaired workers to follow the instruction in real time. The visual format also supports all learners, as captions reinforce key points and can be reviewed at a personal pace.

To implement this approach, I recommend that training designers embed accurate, well-timed captions into existing video content and use clear, concise language. Supporting visuals such as icons for common allergens and step-by-step graphics further enhance comprehension. Hosting the modules on a learning management system enables staff to access them on demand and track completion.

When training is truly accessible, compliance improves and staff confidence grows. Inclusive allergen safety education not only protects guests with food sensitivities but also demonstrates a commitment to equity within the hospitality workforce.
