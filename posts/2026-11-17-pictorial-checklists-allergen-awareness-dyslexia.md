---
title: "Pictorial Checklists for Allergen Awareness with Employees With Dyslexia"
date: 2026-11-17
summary: "Using clear picture-based checklists helps employees with dyslexia reliably follow allergen safety steps."
---

![](images/headers/clarity.svg)

Allergen awareness is a core component of food safety, and it must be accessible to every team member. Employees with dyslexia often find dense text instructions difficult to process quickly, which can increase the risk of cross-contact in a busy kitchen.

Pictorial checklists translate each step of allergen handling into simple images that convey the same information without relying on long sentences. By pairing a photo of a labeled ingredient container with a symbol for “no-touch” and a visual cue for proper storage, the checklist becomes a quick reference that can be scanned at the point of use.

In practice, the checklist is posted at each prep station and laminated for durability. Workers follow the sequence: locate the picture of the ingredient, verify the color-coded allergen label, and then check the next image that shows the correct storage location. A brief pause to confirm the visual cue reinforces the habit without requiring reading comprehension.

When the kitchen adopts pictorial checklists, the whole team benefits. Errors related to allergen cross-contact decline, and employees with dyslexia experience greater confidence and independence. The approach also supports a culture of inclusion, because the visual system is useful for all staff, not only those with learning differences.
